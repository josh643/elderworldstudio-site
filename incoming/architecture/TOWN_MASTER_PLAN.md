## District phasing
- **Phase 0 (now):** campus exterior to the 10-year reference + lobby to the cutaway.
- **Phase 1, District 1:** 12 lots on Elder Avenue (commercial) and Forge Street (mixed), the 3 blocks on the campus axis. Open nearest-first: L05, L06 first.
- **Phase 2:** next ring of blocks east/west on the avenue (pads already marked in the reserve). Open when District 1 is >= 60% leased/built.

## Streets and transit
- Elder Avenue = main spine (campus front). Forge Street parallel at +65 m. Cross lanes every 65 m.
- Transit proposal: a looping tram/shuttle stop at the campus plaza + one per district (cosmetic; agents/visitors can fast-travel). Bike lanes on the avenue later.
