What testers actually do

This is unpaid, optional, and honest about what we need.

  • Install early Android builds from Google Play closed testing.
  • Play for a few minutes (or longer if you enjoy it).
  • Tell us about crashes, confusing screens, balance issues, or anything that feels off.
  • You can leave the group or opt out of a test any time in Play Console / Play Store.

What we will not do

  • We will not charge you to test.
  • We will not require a review or rating.
  • We will not run paid ads to recruit testers.
  • Test builds may show ads (Unity Ads test mode or live ads later). Say so if that is a problem for you.
  • Builds can be rough, incomplete, or wipe progress between versions.
