What’s inside

  • 100 props plus 49 modular parts: containers, farm, food, goods, ingots, ores, smithy, storage, tavern and timber
  • Real-world scale, 2K PBR textures (base colour, normal, metallic/smoothness), one material per prop, prefabs with colliders
  • Modular open crates (16 contents, half or full), barrels with adjustable liquid, bottles and vials with fill levels
  • Demo scene with every prop laid out by category
  • Tested in Unity 6000.3 (Built-in). URP supported via Unity’s material converter.

Some meshes were created with the help of AI tools (LCM Dreamshaper v7 reference images and Microsoft TRELLIS, MIT license), then cleaned, retopologised, re-textured and checked by hand. Others are procedurally modelled in Blender. Full details are in the included README.
